AutoTermin is a modern, privacy-focused mobile application built with Jetpack Compose for comprehensive vehicle maintenance and cost tracking.

Key Features:
• Complete Fleet Management: Keep track of multiple vehicles (cars, motorcycles, trailers) in one place.
• Schedule & Expiry Monitoring: Never miss mandatory technical inspections, insurance renewals, or tire changes.
• Service & Repair Logs: Record workshop visits, track repair history, and log expenses with multi-currency support.
• Local PDF Reports: Generate and export detailed vehicle history reports locally without cloud dependencies.
• 100% Offline & Private: All data is stored securely on your device with no required online account or telemetry.

Active Development & Support:
This project is actively maintained, with long-term updates and feature expansion planned based on community adoption and user feedback.
